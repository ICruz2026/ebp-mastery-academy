Module 2 memory map

Descriptive

No manipulated intervention; describes or observes.

Quasi-experimental

Intervention but no random assignment.

Experimental

Intervention and random assignment; strongest causal inference.

IRB

Protects participants' rights, safety, and welfare before research starts.
